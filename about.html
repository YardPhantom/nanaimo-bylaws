Nanaimo Bylaw Tracker is an independent civic-information website created by Hesh co. It brings the City of Nanaimo’s publicly available bylaws into one searchable place and helps residents follow amendments, consolidations, repeals, replacements, and bylaws currently being discussed by Council.

The site keeps timestamped preserved snapshots of collected records and source documents so changes can be compared over time. Visitors can browse by category, search by number or topic, save one personal watchlist in their browser, and manage subscription preferences. Each record links back to its official City source whenever one is available.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Nanaimo. Official City records remain the authoritative source. The tracker is designed to make those records easier to find, understand, and monitor for the general public.

The City of Nanaimo does not post all of its bylaws online.

What the tracker covers

Current records

Publicly listed Nanaimo bylaws, descriptions, categories, dates, statuses, and source links.

Change history

Timestamped snapshots and detected additions, changes, and removals between collection runs.

Civic meeting activity

Collected Council decisions and clearly labelled committee, board, commission, and panel recommendations with their source documents.
