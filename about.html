Primary source

The collection process reads the City of Nanaimo’s public bylaw index and public documents made available through the third-party eSCRIBE meeting service. It preserves available titles, numbers, descriptions, categories, statuses, source URLs, meeting references, and PDF locations. The City and eSCRIBE source records—not this tracker—remain the ultimate authority.

Nanaimo Bylaw Tracker is an independent civic-information website created by Hesh co. It brings publicly available Nanaimo bylaws, related Council activity, and committee, board, commission, and panel records into one searchable archive.

The tracker is intended to make scattered public records easier to locate and compare. Visitors can search by bylaw number, title, topic, category, year, department, status, or source; follow amendments, consolidations, repeals, and replacements; open preserved source documents; and review the civic meeting activity connected to a record.

Collection runs create timestamped snapshots and compare them with earlier records. This helps identify additions, changed descriptions, altered links, new meeting references, and other detectable revisions. Preserved source PDFs provide a stable reference when public meeting systems later reorganize or remove links.

The archive reflects only information that was publicly available and successfully collected. The City of Nanaimo does not publish every bylaw online, and a missing record does not prove that a bylaw is invalid, inactive, repealed, or unavailable through another official channel.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by the City of Nanaimo or eSCRIBE. Official City records and the underlying eSCRIBE source data remain authoritative. Users making legal, property, licensing, financial, or compliance decisions should confirm the current record directly with the City.

How the archive works

Collect

Automated collection checks public City and eSCRIBE sources for bylaw records, meeting pages, agenda material, minutes, and linked documents.

Preserve

Available metadata and source PDFs are stored with stable archive paths, collection timestamps, and duplicate-content checks.

Compare

New collection results are compared with earlier snapshots so additions, amendments, removals, link changes, and other revisions can be surfaced.

What the tracker covers

Current records

Publicly listed Nanaimo bylaws with available descriptions, categories, dates, statuses, departments, official links, and archived document references.

Change history

Timestamped snapshots and detected additions, amendments, consolidations, repeals, replacements, removals, and source-link changes between collection runs.

Civic meeting activity

Extracted Council decisions and separately labelled committee, board, commission, and panel recommendations, motions, and supporting source documents.

Coverage, verification, and privacy

Coverage: The archive can only include records exposed by the public sources being collected. Historical gaps, inaccessible documents, changed URLs, and incomplete source metadata may remain.

Verification: Automated relationship and status detection helps organize records but can require human review. Every available record keeps a route back to its source so users can verify the current official wording.

Personal tools: The watchlist is stored locally unless a signed-in user chooses account synchronization. Subscription and account features are separate from the public bylaw archive.
